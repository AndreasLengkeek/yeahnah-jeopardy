# Clue Card flips as one object, not two separate cards

When the Host reveals an Answer, the Clue Card that zoomed in from the Board flips over as a single object — front face shows Category/Value/Clue text, back face shows the Answer — rather than a second, separate card appearing alongside or below it. The Category/Value header, buzzed-player status, and the Host's action buttons (Reveal, and later Correct/Incorrect) stay fixed outside the flipping face rather than riding along on either side, so they never rotate out of view or stop being clickable mid-animation. Chosen for a simpler component model, and because the original design canvas's Reveal button was already labeled "Flip tile — show answer," implying one card turning over rather than a second card being dealt.
